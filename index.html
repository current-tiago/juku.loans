When two business loans are cheaper at different rate types, a comparative advantage swap lets both get the loan they wanted for less. You bring the client, we handle the swap.

Inside a swap

  1. Your client wants a fixed rate, but its best deal is floating at SOFR + 1.20%, so it borrows floating. The other business is the opposite: it wants floating but borrows fixed at 6.80%.

  2. Through our partner bank, your client pays a fixed rate and receives floating, which offsets its floating loan. The other business does the reverse.

  3. Your client ends up paying 7.00% fixed instead of the 7.20% it was quoted, and the other business saves the same 0.20% a year. You earn a commission on the deal.
